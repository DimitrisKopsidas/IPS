Crystal - An Interactive Promotional Display

Crystal is a future commercial app whose aim is to provide interactivity in DOOH (Digital Out-of-Home) advertising screens. 
The app consists of 3 main applications
1) A product page which displays discounts in the form of an interactive carousel, which after a certain number of actions will offer a coupon to a customer after playing a minigame.
2) An administrating page in which the store owner will be able to control all the product pages in his store and get access to information gathered by the interactions of the customers with the advertising screens
3) A microcomputer module with a built in camera that will be able to connect to any HDMI compatible screen or monitor turning it to an interactive display
